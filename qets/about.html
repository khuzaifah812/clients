About Queen Elizabeth Tours & Safaris

A local team, founded in 2023, based right inside Queen Elizabeth National Park.

Our story

Founded by Kasoke Julius, 2023.

We are dedicated nature conservationists. Founded by Kasoke Julius in 2023, we are a tour operator and guide based in Katunguru, Kazinga Channel, Kasese Municipality in western Uganda. We organise group and private tours for our clients and offer a wide range of tourism products such as game drives, boat cruises, accommodation, chimpanzee tracking, gorilla tracking, cultural tourism, bird watching, Rwenzori mountains trekking and many others.

Every itinerary is built around the traveller in front of us — whether that's a solo backpacker after one unforgettable boat cruise, or a family planning a full week across the park.
