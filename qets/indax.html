About us

Dedicated nature conservationists.

Founded by Kasoke Julius in 2023, we are a tour operator and guide based in Katunguru, Kazinga Channel, Kasese Municipality in western Uganda. We organise group and private tours and offer a wide range of tourism products — game drives, boat cruises, accommodation, chimpanzee tracking, gorilla tracking, cultural tourism, bird watching, Rwenzori mountains trekking and many others.

What we offer

19 ways to experience the park

From game drives and boat cruises to gorilla tracking and agritourism. Every trip can be private or shared with a group.

01

Game drive

Guided drives through the park to view lions, leopards, elephants, buffaloes and antelopes.

03

Boat cruise

A guided cruise on the Kazinga Channel, close to hippos, crocodiles and elephants along the shore.

07

Gorilla tracking

Trek and see gorilla families in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest National Park.

06

Bird watching

Fish eagles, pelicans, flamingos, kingfishers and yellow-billed storks across the park.

05

Rwenzori trekking

See snow-capped Margherita Peak, duikers, Johnston's chameleon and varied vegetation zones.

04

Cultural tourism

Traditional dance, healers, blacksmiths, storytellers and basket weaving.
